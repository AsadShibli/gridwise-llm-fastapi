Operator notes in. Cheapest safe schedule out.

A campus runs on grid, rooftop solar and a battery. Operators leave short notes like “wash the panels from noon to 2 PM”. GridWise reads them with an LLM, checks the result, and solves a linear program for the lowest-cost 24-hour plan.
